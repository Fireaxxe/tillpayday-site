How the figure works

Till Payday takes what is in your account, subtracts any bills still due before your next payday, and divides what is left by the days until then. That is your figure for today. Logging a spend lowers it.

A day's allowance is set by what was in the account that morning. Spending does not shrink it further during the day. Tomorrow it is worked out again from what is left.

Common questions

The figure looks too low

Check Bills. Anything still due before payday comes off before the division, so the figure is what is genuinely free to spend.

I got paid early

Settings, then Paid early this time. That replaces the rule for this cycle only, and clears itself once the date passes.

My payday is not a fixed date

Settings offers the last Friday of the month, the last working day, a fixed day of the month, or every 7, 14 or 28 days.

I typed the wrong amount

Tap the entry under today's spending, or open the spending history, and correct or delete it. Your balance moves with it.

Money came in mid month

Use Add money under the log button. It raises your balance without counting as something you spent.

It is not syncing between my devices

There is no sync. Everything stays on the device you entered it on.

I want to start over

Settings, then Reset everything.
